🛡️ SPEAK Speech Quality Gate

This prototype tests whether a transcript is usable before it reaches the learning validator.

Practice task:
Tell the travel agent where you would like to go.

Example: I'd like to visit Japan.

Status: Ready

⚪ NO SPEECH — waiting for speech
Gate rule for this prototype

🟢 CLEAR — a usable transcript was returned and contains enough speech to continue to semantic validation.

🟡 UNCERTAIN — transcription failed, is extremely short, or contains no usable words. Ask the learner to say it again. No penalty and no hint increase.

⚪ NO SPEECH — nothing has been submitted yet.

Important: CLEAR does not mean the learner is correct. It only means the transcript is usable enough to send to the next validation layer.
Prototype only. Audio is recorded temporarily in the browser and sent to the speech backend for transcription. This page does not intentionally save the audio recording. Do not use personal information during testing.
